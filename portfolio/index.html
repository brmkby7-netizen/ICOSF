04 — About

Behind the wheel.

I'm Bahri — an Unreal Engine tech artist, virtual production operator and the person behind The Green Goat.

My work sits where cars and cinema meet. I build the scene, rig the car, set the lights and move the camera until the frame feels like the opening shot of a launch film. Everything is made in Unreal Engine 5 and Blender, which means fast iterations for you and final-quality pixels at the end.

Whether it's a single hero still or a full cinematic, I care about the same things a car photographer does: clean reflections, honest materials and a silhouette that reads in half a second.
