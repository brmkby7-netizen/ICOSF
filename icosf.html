Nurturing the Whole Child Mind, Body, Family

At the Islamic Center of South Florida, our goal has always been to raise children who are grounded in their faith, strong in their character, and confident in who they are. Our weekend programs — Tarbiya Academy and the Junior Youth Program — nurture the whole child: a mind rooted in Quran, Arabic, and Islamic Studies; a body kept active through organized, supervised sport; and a family that learns and grows together.

Quran Hifdh

AGES 7–16

Hifdh is far more than memorization. It is about helping your child build an intimate, lasting relationship with the word of Allah — to internalize it, live with it, and truly love it. As they commit the Quran to heart, they also learn its meanings and how it speaks to their own lives, so what they memorize becomes something they understand and carry.

For students who can read from the Mushaf independently.

Can be taken on its own for families focusing only on Hifdh — or combined with Tarbiya Academy or Junior Youth for a complete experience.

Junior Youth

AGES 11–13

For pre-teens, how they learn matters as much as what they learn. Junior Youth leans into creative, discussion-based learning — where young people ask real questions, explore real answers, and connect their faith to daily life — supported by mentors who guide, listen, and model what it means to grow into a confident young Muslim.
